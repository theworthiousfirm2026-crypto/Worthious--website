About Worthious

A simpler way to prepare everyday meals

The Worthious Firm is a Gurugram-based start-up founded in 2026. Under the supervision of Mrs. Sushma Sharma and Mr. Deepak Sharma, we aim to provide quality products and convenient service for everyday kitchens.
